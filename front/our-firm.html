Our firm

Delivering first-class, innovative, tailored and independent actuarial solutions since 2014.

Overview

Actuarial Factor, LLC is an international and independent actuarial consulting firm, founded in 2014, with the purpose of providing tailored actuarial services with the highest quality standards and expertise in every line of business across all jurisdictions in the United States, Latin America, and the Caribbean. Our clients include property and casualty (re)insurance companies, life and health (re)insurance companies, managing general agents (MGAs), insurtechs, self-insured entities, and captive insurance companies.

The Story Behind Actuarial Factor

In the summer of 2014, my family took a two-week road trip from San Diego to San Francisco. As we drove, I filled a notebook with ideas for the name of my new company. I read each one aloud, and my 10-year-old son never hesitated to share his honest opinion: “No.”

As our trip came to an end, I started to wonder whether I would ever find a name he liked. Then I said, “Actuarial Factor.” This time, my son approved.

When I got home, the first thing I did was pull out the Oxford English Dictionary and look up the word factor. One of the definitions described taking something complex and making it simpler. I loved it. The meaning felt like a perfect reflection of what I wanted my company to do: help clients understand complex actuarial matters by making them clear and simple. To this day, that promise remains at the heart of Actuarial Factor.
