About Actuarial Factor

Actuarial Factor is an independent actuarial consulting firm, founded in 2014, with the mission of delivering first-class quality, innovative, tailored, expert and independent actuarial solutions for our clients to comply with the insurance laws and regulations and exceed their business needs.

We serve every line of business across all jurisdictions in the United States (including Puerto Rico), Latin America, and the Caribbean. We specialize in property and casualty (re)insurance companies, self-insured corporations and captive insurance companies.
